### EP Certification Mark Quality Control Standards Summary

#### certification mark defines the quality standards for Electronic Shelf Label (ESL) accessories such as rails, adapters, and other attachment hardware. This document outlines the rigorous criteria for products to qualify for the "EP" mark, focusing on craftsmanship, compatibility, durability, and overall performance.

#### The EP

#### Quality of Craftsmanship:

•

#### Materials: High-quality, durable, and environmentally compliant materials are required.

•

#### Manufacturing: Adherence to ISO 9001 standards for precision and consistency.

•

#### Finish and Detailing: Superior finish with no defects and uniform surface treatments.

•

#### Dimensional Accuracy: Precise tolerances to ensure compatibility and functionality.

#### Compatibility:

•

#### ESL Tag Compatibility: Must be compatible with a wide range of ESL tags.

•

#### Mounting and Attachment: Secure and stable mounting for easy installation and removal.

•

#### Interoperability: Must work with existing store fixtures and shelving systems.

#### Durability and Reliability:

•

#### Load Bearing: Must support the weight of ESL tags and promotional materials.

•

#### Environmental Resistance: Resistant to humidity, temperature variations, and UV exposure.

•

#### Longevity: Long service life, withstanding frequent handling and cleaning.

#### Testing and Certification:

•

#### Initial Testing: Rigorous mechanical, environmental, and compatibility testing for new designs.

•

#### Ongoing QA: Regular quality checks on production batches.

•

#### Certification: Products meeting standards receive the EP

#### mark; any design/material changes require re-evaluation.

<!-- page 7 -->

#### Documentation and Traceability:

•

#### Technical Documentation: Detailed records including material specs, design drawings, and test reports.

•

#### Traceability: Unique identification numbers or batch codes for tracking.

#### Compliance with Regulatory Standards:

•

#### Safety and Environmental Regulations: Must comply with relevant safety and environmental standards, including RoHS.

#### Review and Update of Standards:

•

#### Periodic Review: Regular updates to maintain alignment with technological advancements and industry best practices.

•

#### Stakeholder Feedback: Consideration of feedback from retailers, manufacturers, and users.

#### These standards ensure that products bearing the EP

#### certification mark provide consistent, reliable, and high-quality performance, enhancing customer satisfaction and trust.
